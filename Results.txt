RESULTS

* Successfully developed a real-time drone detection framework using deep learning and motion analysis.
* Improved classification reliability by incorporating motion-based refinement techniques.
* Reduced false detections caused by visually similar flying objects such as birds.
* Achieved stable real-time performance on multiple surveillance video samples.
* Successfully integrated ESP32-based alert mechanisms for immediate threat notification.
* Enabled automated Telegram notifications along with visual and acoustic alerts.
* Demonstrated practical applicability for surveillance and security-focused environments.
* Validated the end-to-end system from detection to alert generation in real-world testing scenarios.
